# Their time

See your time next to anywhere in the world. Pick a university or city, drag the line, and see their time, their working hours and their mornings against your day. Deadlines given in a school's own time show in yours, with a countdown.

Static site, no server. Places and deadlines stay in your browser.

Credits: [tz-lookup](https://github.com/photostructure/tz-lookup) (CC0) for time zones from coordinates, [Photon](https://photon.komoot.io) and OpenStreetMap for place search, Wikidata for university websites, [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icons (ISC), [Geist](https://vercel.com/font) font (OFL).
